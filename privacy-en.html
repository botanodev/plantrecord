Botano - Plant Growth Journal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026
Last updated: 24 September 2026

In one sentence

Everything you create in this app — your photos, plants, care records and reminder groups — stays on your own phone. We have no server, no account system, and no way to see any of it.

What you create

Photos you import or take, plant names, species, acquisition dates, notes, care records, groups and watering schedules are written to this app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

There is no sign-in and no cloud sync. If you uninstall the app, or clear its data from the system settings, that content is permanently deleted and we cannot recover it for you.

This app is also excluded from Android's automatic backup. That is deliberate: the system backup has a size limit that would restore your records without your photos, leaving you with a library of broken images. Use the app's own backup feature instead.

About backup files

The backup feature packages your database and photos into a single .zip file. You choose where it goes through the system file picker — your device, a cloud drive, wherever you like.

We never upload it, and once it leaves the app we have no access to it. If you store it in a cloud service, that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Photos saved to your gallery

Two features write a copy of an image into your device's gallery, under Pictures/PlantRecord/:

  • Taking a photo inside the app. The photo is saved to your gallery as well as to the app. This is a safety measure — because the app is excluded from system backup, a photo that existed only inside the app would be lost if you ever cleared its data.
  • Saving an exported image (the long image or the grid collage), when you tap Save.

Those copies belong to you and stay on your device. Deleting them from your gallery does not affect the app, and vice versa.

Network connections

The app connects to the internet for two reasons only: to load advertisements, and to verify a membership purchase. Your photos and records are never part of any network request.

Third-party service: Google AdMob (advertising)

Free users see banner advertisements, and an optional rewarded video can be watched to unlock image export for 24 hours. These are served by Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising identifiers in order to deliver and measure ads. That data is collected by Google, not by us — we never see it.

In regions that require it (the EEA, the UK and others), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. If you decline, you will still see ads, but non-personalised ones.

You can reset your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Advertisements are never shown on the image preview or export screens.

Payment data when you buy membership

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail. What we receive is the answer to a single question: whether this device has an active purchase.

No third-party purchase service

Purchase verification happens directly between this app and Google Play. No other company receives your receipt, and none takes any part in deciding whether you own the membership.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy. When a purchase is refunded, the associated benefits are withdrawn.

Feedback

If you email us, we will have whatever you choose to put in that email. We use it to answer you and to fix the problem, and nothing else.

What the app is allowed to do on your phone

Each of these exists to make a feature work. None of them is used to collect information about you.

  • Send notifications — watering reminders need this to appear at all. Without it they are scheduled but never show up.
  • Schedule something for an exact time — so a reminder set for 8:00 arrives at 8:00. Without it the reminder still arrives, just later.
  • Reschedule after a restart — Android clears every scheduled reminder when the device restarts; this is what puts yours back.
  • Use the internet — only to load ads and to confirm a purchase.
  • The advertising identifier — used by Google AdMob to deliver and measure ads. This is the only identifier tied to you here, and you can reset it at any time in your device settings.

Beyond those there are a few technical permissions that come bundled with the notification and advertising libraries — keeping the device awake for the moment a notification is delivered, for example. They collect nothing.

The app does not request permission to read your photo library or to use your camera. Importing photos goes through the system photo picker, and taking a photo hands you over to your own camera app — in both cases your selection is the authorisation, so no permission prompt is needed and the app never gains access to the rest of your library.

What we do not do

  • We do not have an account system, and never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not upload your photos or records anywhere.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not show interstitial (full-screen) advertisements.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in complete control of it. Delete individual photos or plants inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings.

There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never held anything. Data held by Google AdMob and Google Play is governed by their own policies.
